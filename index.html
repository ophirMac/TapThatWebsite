How It Works

Three steps. No app download required. Just your phone and your favorite spot.

01

Tap or Scan

Walk into any participating venue. Tap your phone on the NFC reader, or let the cashier scan your QR code. You're instantly enrolled.

02

Collect Stamps

Watch your digital stamp card fill up in real time. Track your progress from your phone, Apple Wallet, or just ask the cashier.

03

Unlock Rewards

Hit the stamp goal and claim your reward instantly. Free coffee, discounts, surprises. No paper cards, no hassle, no forgetting.
